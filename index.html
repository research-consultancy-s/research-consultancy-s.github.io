Services

Two ways to work together

Pick whichever fits. Most clients start with consulting and move into a technical collaboration once the direction is clear.

Technical collaboration

I run the simulations and analysis with you

  • Neural network simulations MNIST and Fashion-MNIST benchmarks, including hardware-aware training and memristor / neuromorphic device models with realistic non-idealities.
  • DFT calculations Structure relaxation, electronic structure, density of states, formation energies and defect chemistry using Quantum ESPRESSO.
  • Machine-learning interatomic potentials Dataset construction, MLIP training and validation, then production molecular-dynamics runs in LAMMPS.
  • Data analysis & publication figures Turning raw measurement or simulation output into clean, journal-ready figures with a defensible interpretation.

Consulting & mentoring

I advise, review and guide, and you stay the author

  • Finding a publishable topic Scoping a question that is genuinely novel, achievable with the equipment and time you actually have, and likely to clear peer review.
  • Review papers & literature strategy Systematic literature search, framing the gap, and an outline that makes a review paper worth a journal's attention.
  • Manuscript & journal submission Structure, argument, language editing, journal selection, cover letter, and reviewer-response strategy.
  • Thesis planning & chapter feedback Chapter-by-chapter structure and detailed written feedback, from proposal through to defence preparation.
  • Proposals & one-on-one guidance Research and project proposal drafting and review, plus ongoing mentoring for students starting out in device research.

On academic integrity. I edit, advise and teach. I do not ghostwrite theses, assignments, or anything you will be assessed on. You remain the author of your own work.

Where I make a substantial intellectual contribution to a paper, it is declared openly, normally as co-corresponding, second or third author, agreed in writing before the work starts. Where you would rather commission the technical work as a paid service without adding me as an author, that is also possible and is priced separately. Work that would breach your institution's rules is the one thing I turn down.

About

Who you'd be working with

I completed my PhD in Semiconductor Engineering at Chungbuk National University, South Korea, on easily fabricated and highly stable non-volatile RRAM for neuromorphic computing. Since then I have held postdoctoral research positions at UNIST, working on VO2-based neuron devices for leaky integrate-and-fire models, and at the Korea Institute of Energy Technology (KENTECH), on compound-eye mimicking neuromorphic semiconductors and optoelectronic RRAM built from quantum-dot materials. I am now Assistant Professor in Neuromorphic Computing and Semiconductor Devices at Gachon University.

My work spans both ends of the problem: fabricating and electrically characterising real devices, and the simulation side: DFT, machine-learning interatomic potentials and hardware-aware neural network models. That combination is the reason this consulting exists. Students are often strong on one side and stranded on the other, and the gap between a working device and a publishable story is where most projects stall.

My earlier training was in physics. I took an MPhil at Women University Multan on the optical properties of diamond-like semiconductors, and before that an MSc and BSc in Pakistan. I taught undergraduate physics for four years before moving into research full time, which is where the mentoring half of this work comes from.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Who is this actually for?

Master's and PhD students, postdocs, and small research groups working on resistive switching, memristors, neuromorphic hardware, or materials simulation. Also research groups who need a specific calculation or simulation done properly and don't have anyone in-house who has done it before.

What happens on the free call?

Twenty minutes, video or voice, whichever you prefer. You explain the problem; I ask questions and tell you what I think it needs. If I'm not the right person, I'll say so and point you elsewhere where I can. There is no pitch and no obligation.

How is co-authorship decided?

Before the work starts, in writing. If I make a substantial intellectual contribution to a paper, such as designing the simulations, interpreting the results or writing sections, co-authorship is appropriate, and we agree the position up front: usually co-corresponding, second or third author.

Students get 50% off the collaboration fee when I am included as co-corresponding author. If you would rather not add an author at all, you can commission the same technical work as a paid service instead, priced separately, since authorship is part of what makes a collaboration worth taking on at the collaboration rate.

I will never ask for authorship on work I did not genuinely contribute to, and I will not accept it either.

Can you guarantee my paper gets published?

No, and nobody honest can. Peer review is not something any consultant controls. What I can do is make the work more defensible and the manuscript much harder to reject for fixable reasons: weak framing, unclear figures, poor journal fit, thin interpretation.

How long do things take?

Manuscript editing and chapter feedback typically return within one to two weeks. Review paper packages take around three weeks. Simulation collaborations depend entirely on scope and queue time. A bounded DFT study is usually four to eight weeks. You get a specific timeline in the written plan before you pay anything.

Is my unpublished work kept confidential?

Yes. Unpublished data, manuscripts and ideas you share stay confidential by default, and I'm glad to sign your institution's NDA if you have one. I don't reuse client data in my own work.

How do payments work?

An invoice goes out before work begins. Fixed-scope work is 50% up front and 50% on delivery; longer collaborations are billed monthly. International bank transfer and the usual online transfer services both work, and we'll settle the method that is cheapest for you when the quote is agreed.

Which timezones do you cover?

I'm based in South Korea (KST, UTC+9) and regularly meet clients in Europe, South Asia and North America. Early mornings and evenings KST are both available, so most timezones have a workable overlap.
